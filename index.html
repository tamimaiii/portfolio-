          Dubai resale transaction

          1. Form A listing agreementThe seller signs a RERA Form A with the brokerage. The ad needs a Trakheesi permit number before it goes live.
          2. Form B and offerA buyer's brokerage agreement (Form B) covers the buyer side. Price, deposit and conditions are agreed in writing.
          3. Form F memorandum of understandingBuyer and seller sign the Form F MOU. A deposit of usually 10% is held, often as a cheque with the brokerage.
          4. Developer NOCThe seller requests a no-objection certificate from the developer, which confirms service charges are clear. Mortgaged units also need the bank's release process.
          5. Trustee office transferBoth parties attend a DLD registration trustee centre with manager's cheques. The 4% fee and trustee fees are paid and the new title deed is issued.
          6. Handover and utilitiesKeys, DEWA connection, Ejari if let, and the buyer's Golden Visa application if the value qualifies.
